About Lendme

Lendme is a student finance project for tertiary students in Botswana. Create an account, upload your documents, request an amount and follow your application status.

Frequently asked questions

What is the interest rate?

30% per year, calculated monthly on the remaining balance. Use the calculator to estimate your payments.

Which documents are needed?

Student ID, proof of registration, results and a bank statement (PDF, JPG or PNG).

What does Pending mean?

Your application was submitted and has not been reviewed yet. Approved means it was accepted. Rejected means it was declined.

Is my information safe?

Passwords are stored hashed and documents are kept in private storage, visible only to Lendme staff.
