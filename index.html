Lendme is a student loan platform concept for tertiary students in Botswana. Create an account, upload your documents and track your application in one place.

How it works

  1. Create your account. Sign up with your email.
  2. Upload your documents. Add what is needed and sign.
  3. Request an amount. Enter how much you need.
  4. Track your status. See if your application is Pending or Approved.
